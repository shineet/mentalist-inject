Does it play with nobody touching it?

One tap to arm, then hands off. Each test fires itself 5 seconds later and reports whether real sound is coming out. Run this on a phone that has never played YouTube in Safari, or the answer is meaningless.

1 · Our own audio, Apple preview

The tap plays silence on an audio element, which blesses it. The real song is swapped in afterwards with no touch. 30-second preview from Apple, free and CORS-open.

idle

2 · YouTube embed, blessed then swapped

The tap starts a throwaway video inside a real YouTube player. The spectator's song is then loaded into that same player with no touch. If this passes, the screen is genuinely YouTube.

idle

3 · YouTube embed, muted then unmuted

Muted autoplay is always allowed. The question is whether the tap buys the right to unmute later, which is the part iOS normally refuses.
