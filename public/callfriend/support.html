CallFriend Support

CallFriend is a dialler for live performers. You set one contact in advance, and the keypad places a call to them through your own Twilio account. It is a self-contained performance tool, not a replacement for your phone's dialler, and it places no ordinary calls.

Setting it up

  1. Open Settings and enter the contact to reach and, if you want, a recorded greeting.
  2. Give your own Twilio account details, which place the call.
  3. Tap a number on the keypad and press Call. The call is routed to your contact or greeting; the number shown is never dialled.

App Clip

A companion App Clip lets you run the dialler on a borrowed phone with nothing to install, reached from a link or code. It reaches the same contact and has no access to that phone's Contacts.
