CallFriend Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

CallFriend has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. This page lists everything it does with information.

What the dialler does

CallFriend shows a telephone dialler used inside a staged performance. You set one contact in advance. A call placed from the keypad is routed by your own Twilio account to that contact, or to a recorded greeting. The number typed on the keypad is cosmetic and is never dialled; no third party is ever called. Which keys are pressed is never recorded and never leaves the phone.

The room

If a room name is used, the performer's app sends the routine's settings (the contact to reach and the greeting) to our server at mindgames.fly.dev, filed under that room name. The phone running the routine reads those settings to place the call. The phone sends back only which screen it is on. No typed digits, no names, no contact details from the device, no device identifiers and no location are sent.

The performer's app may also send a still image of a dialler to the room, which the phone displays. It contains no personal data. The server keeps each room's settings and latest status only, passes them between the two phones, and never sells, shares or uses them for advertising.

Without a room name, CallFriend sends nothing anywhere.

Sample contacts

The contacts and recent calls shown are fictional, bundled with the app as stage dressing, with openly licensed stock photographs. No contact data is read from the device. The App Clip has no access to your Contacts at all.

Calls, microphone and camera

Calls are carried by Twilio under the performer's own account and are not recorded or stored by this app. The microphone is used only during a call. In the App Clip, the camera is used only during a video call. Audio and video are not recorded by CallFriend.

Deleting data

Deleting CallFriend removes everything stored on your iPhone. App Clips are removed by iOS automatically after a period of not being used. To have a room deleted from the server, email us with its room name.

Children

CallFriend is not directed at children and collects no personal information from them knowingly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
