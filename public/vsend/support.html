Vsend Support

Voice notes, your own links, and a calculator you can share.

Contact

Email shine@texasmentalist.com. Replies usually within two working days.

Voice notes say they are not available

Vsend recognises speech on your iPhone only. If your language has not been downloaded for on-device use, go to Settings > General > Keyboard and turn on Dictation, then try again. Vsend also needs Microphone and Speech Recognition turned on under Settings > Vsend.

Making a destination

In Destinations, tap +, give it a name, and write a link with {TEXT} where your words should go. For example https://www.google.com/search?q={TEXT} searches Google for what you said.

Leaving the calculator

Press and hold the time at the top of the calculator.

Using the calculator on a friend's phone

In Calculator, tap New next to Room, then Send these settings. Your friend scans the code with their Camera and the calculator opens as an App Clip, with nothing to install. Send the settings again after changing anything. Turn on "Show results from that phone" to see what it works out.
