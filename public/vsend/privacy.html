Vsend Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Vsend has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. This page lists everything it does with information, part by part.

Voice notes

Speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition on your iPhone. Vsend requires on-device recognition, so your voice and the text are not sent to Apple, to us or to anyone else. Nothing is recorded or kept after you clear the note.

Destinations

The links you create are stored only on your iPhone. When you tap one, Vsend opens that link with your text in it, in whichever app or website handles it. What happens next is governed by that app or website's own privacy policy.

Calculator on a friend's phone

This is the only part of Vsend that uses a server. When you choose "Send these settings", Vsend sends the calculator settings you chose to our server at mindgames.fly.dev, filed under the room name you picked. When the calculator then runs on another phone through the App Clip, it sends back what it works out:

  • the number of days alive, and the date of birth that was entered, if three numbers were labelled as a date of birth
  • the totals of calculations

Nothing else is sent: no name, no contact details, no device identifiers, no location. The data is filed under the room name only and is not linked to you or to the other person's identity.

The server keeps each room's current settings and its last 20 results, and nothing older. It is used only to pass these figures between the two phones. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

If you do not use this feature, Vsend sends nothing anywhere.

Deleting data

Deleting Vsend removes everything stored on your iPhone. To have a room deleted from the server, email us with its room name.

Children

Vsend is not directed at children and collects no personal information from them knowingly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
