Mind Unlock Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Mind Unlock has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. This page lists everything it does with information.

The passcode screen

Mind Unlock shows a passcode screen as part of a magic trick. No real passcode is ever asked for, entered, stored or sent. The performer types made-up digits; which keys are pressed is never recorded and never leaves the phone. The phone it runs on is never locked or unlocked by it, and it cannot read or change the phone's real passcode.

The remote

This is the only part of Mind Unlock that uses a server. If a room name is used, the performer's app sends the trick's settings (how many tries fail, 4 or 6 digits, a background colour, Face ID or Touch ID) to our server at mindgames.fly.dev, filed under that room name. The phone running the trick reads those settings and sends back only:

  • which screen it is showing (blank, black, passcode or unlocked)
  • how many tries have been made, and how many dots are filled in

Nothing else is sent: no digits, no name, no contact details, no device identifiers, no location. The server keeps each room's settings and its latest status only, is used only to pass them between the two phones, and never sells, shares or uses them for advertising.

Without a room name, Mind Unlock sends nothing anywhere.

Opening Google

At the end of the trick, Mind Unlock opens www.google.com in Safari. What happens there is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Deleting data

Deleting Mind Unlock removes everything stored on your iPhone. App Clips are removed by iOS automatically after a period of not being used. To have a room deleted from the server, email us with its room name.

Children

Mind Unlock is not directed at children and collects no personal information from them knowingly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
