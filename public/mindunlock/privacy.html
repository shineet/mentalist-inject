Mind Unlock Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Mind Unlock has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. This page lists everything it does with information.

The passcode screen

Mind Unlock shows a passcode screen as part of a magic trick. No real passcode is ever asked for, entered, stored or sent. On the audience member's phone, which keys are pressed is never recorded and never leaves the phone. The phone it runs on is never locked or unlocked by it, and it cannot read or change the phone's real passcode.

The remote

This is the only part of Mind Unlock that uses a server. If a room name is used, the performer's app sends the trick's settings (how many tries fail, 4 or 6 digits, a background colour, Face ID or Touch ID, a pause before the unlock, and the web page it opens to) to our server at mindgames.fly.dev, filed under that room name. The phone running the trick reads those settings and sends back only:

  • which screen it is showing (blank, black, passcode or unlocked)
  • how many tries have been made, and how many dots are filled in

Nothing else is sent from that phone: no digits, no name, no contact details, no device identifiers, no location. The server keeps each room's settings and its latest status only, is used only to pass them between the two phones, and never sells, shares or uses them for advertising.

Without a room name, Mind Unlock sends nothing anywhere.

Swap (version 1.1 and later)

In Swap, the roles turn round: the performer hands their own phone, running Mind Unlock, to a participant, who makes up a code on the spot and types it. Those made-up digits, and whether each try opened the screen, are passed live through our server to the performer's other phone, so the performer can see them. They are never a real passcode: the phone is the performer's, and the participant invents the code during the trick.

The server holds them in memory only, never on disk, and deletes them after 10 minutes, when the performer resets the trick, or when the server restarts, whichever comes first. They are not linked to any person or device.

The Duo peek display (version 1.1 and later)

The performer's app can connect over Bluetooth to a small display the performer owns (the Duo) and show on it which screen the trick is on and how many tries have been made. It never shows typed digits. Nothing about this leaves the performer's phone except the Bluetooth link to that display.

The page it opens to

At the end of the trick, Mind Unlock opens a web page: www.google.com unless the performer chose another. What happens on that page is governed by that site's own privacy policy.

Deleting data

Deleting Mind Unlock removes everything stored on your iPhone. App Clips are removed by iOS automatically after a period of not being used. To have a room deleted from the server, email us with its room name.

Children

Mind Unlock is not directed at children and collects no personal information from them knowingly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
