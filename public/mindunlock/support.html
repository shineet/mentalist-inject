Mind Unlock Support

Mind Unlock is a prop for magicians and mentalists. On a borrowed phone, a passcode screen refuses the performer's guesses, then "unlocks" when the performer appears to read the owner's mind. The phone is never actually locked, and no real passcode is ever entered.

How it runs

  1. Open the trick on the phone from the performer's link or NFC tag. It shows a blank page.
  2. Tap the top-right corner: the screen goes black, as if asleep.
  3. Tap anywhere: the passcode screen appears.
  4. Each try fails until the number of failures set in the app; the next one unlocks and opens Google.

On the passcode screen, the top-left corner makes the next try unlock, the top-right corner makes it fail, and a tap on the dots switches between 4 and 6 digits. The Mind Unlock app can change all of this live, and has a rehearsal mode.
