ELEVENLABS SCRIPT — interactive routine voice over

TAG SYNTAX
ElevenLabs v3 uses square-bracket audio tags: [short pause], [long pause].
The angle-bracket <break time="..."> form is the v2/turbo syntax and was
ignored by whatever model produced the last take -- every gap came back
between 0.30s and 0.68s, which is why it could not be split.

TRY THIS FIRST: ONE FILE
Paste everything between the ==== markers as a single generation.

The separators are THREE [long pause] tags in a row. That is deliberate. A
single long pause came back at well under a second last time, indistinguishable
from the pauses inside a sentence. Three should accumulate into something
clearly longer. What the splitter needs is not a specific duration, just a gap
between lines that is obviously bigger than any gap inside a line.

Send the file and it will be checked before anything is installed: if the
segment count is not exactly 8, nothing gets written. If the gaps come back
uniform again, fall back to the eight separate generations at the bottom.

ORDER (this is what the splitter assumes -- do not reorder)
  1 intro   2 round1   3 round2   4 round3
  5 round4  6 round5-logo   7 round5-green   8 hold

VOICE SETTINGS
Calm, warm, unhurried. Slightly slower than conversational. These are
instructions spoken to a room over a PA while people search a screen, not
narration read to someone holding a phone. No rising inflection at the ends --
they are instructions, not questions. Keep the level even line to line.

Do not add performance tags like [dramatically] or [excited]. The delivery
should be identical across all eight lines, or the reveal sounds like a
different person from the setup.

====

Look at the screen. [short pause] Take a moment. [short pause] And think of any one of these.

[long pause] [long pause] [long pause]

Now, [short pause] move to the closest one that has a face. [short pause] Not your own. [short pause] If two look equally close, take either.

[long pause] [long pause] [long pause]

Now, [short pause] move to the food nearest to you.

[long pause] [long pause] [long pause]

Now, [short pause] move to the nearest object you could pick up and hold. [short pause] Not food, [short pause] and nothing alive.

[long pause] [long pause] [long pause]

Now, [short pause] move to the animal nearest to you.

[long pause] [long pause] [long pause]

And finally. [short pause] Move to the nearest logo.

[long pause] [long pause] [long pause]

And finally. [short pause] Move to the green thing nearest to you.

[long pause] [long pause] [long pause]

Now stay exactly where you are. [short pause] Don't move. [short pause] Lock it in, [short pause] and keep your eyes on it.

====

NOTE ON ROUND 3
"Not food, and nothing alive" is not padding. Without the first, every carrot
and burger on screen answers the instruction; without the second, so do the
animals. Both exclusions are load-bearing -- the routine failed in performance
because that line was ambiguous.
